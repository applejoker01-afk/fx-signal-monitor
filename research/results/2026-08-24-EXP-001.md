# 2026-08-24 — EXP-001: RSI reversal + MACD confirmation

**Status: hold**

## Hypothesis under test

EXP-001 (research/EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.md): "RSI reversal plus MACD
confirmation improves entry quality."

Fixed candidate rule, as pre-registered: RSI(14) recovers above 40 after
dipping to ≤40 within the prior 10 bars, AND the MACD line crosses above its
signal line, for longs; the mirror condition (RSI recovers below 60 after a
spike to ≥60, MACD crosses below signal) for shorts. Applied as an
additional filter on top of the current production entry gate (TA score
≥60/≤40 agreeing with FA direction ≥55/≤45), matching how the closely
related RSI-only filter is already documented in
`signal_scanner.rsi_oversold_reversal_signal` (2026-08-11).

## Why this run stops at a prerequisite check, not a result

CLAUDE.md requires a *cost-inclusive rolling out-of-sample* test before any
candidate can be compared to baseline. Before writing one, this run checked
whether the repo's data holdings can support that test at all, using
`research/scripts/exp001_data_availability_probe.py` (read-only; imports
`signal_scanner.compute_ta_score`/`rsi`/`macd_now`, does not modify them).

**Target pairs / data cutoff considered:** all 34 pairs in `PAIR_API`
(signal_scanner.py). Data available as of 2026-08-24:

- 33 of 34 pairs: only a live-fetched Frankfurter/ECB daily-close history
  (`signal_scanner.fetch_history_with_dates`, ~200–280 calendar days,
  business-day granularity, no intraday OHLC). No cached long history and no
  point-in-time historical central-bank-rate series exist for these pairs in
  this repo, so a real historical FA gate cannot be reconstructed for them
  going back more than a few months.
- 1 of 34 pairs (USDJPY): a cached 20+ year daily series
  (`data/usdjpy_daily.csv`, 2004-12-31 to 2026-08-21, 5,540 bars) plus cached
  FRED policy-rate series for both legs (`data/fred_cache/usd_dff.csv`,
  `data/fred_cache/jpy_ir3tib.csv`), which is what let a prior ad-hoc script
  (`run_rsi_real_fa_gate_train_test.py`, cited in the `rsi_oversold_reversal_signal`
  docstring) reconstruct a real point-in-time FA gate for that one pair only.

**Probe result (USDJPY, 2004-12-31 – 2026-08-21, start index 200, same
TA≥60/≤40 + FA≥55/≤45 agreement gate as production, one open position at a
time, re-entry allowed 10 bars after each candidate):**

| Filter | Matches |
|---|---|
| Baseline TA+FA-agree candidates (both directions) | 186 |
| + RSI recovery only | 33 |
| + MACD cross only | 20 |
| + RSI recovery AND MACD cross (EXP-001 rule) | **10** (3 in 2004–2019, 7 in 2020–2026; last one, 2026-08-18, has no exit yet) |
| — of which SHORT | **0** |

No cost adjustment, IS/OOS folding, or Reality Check was run on these 10
trades — that would produce a win-rate/PF/expectancy number built on an
n too small to mean anything, which is the "proxy performance claim" this
process is meant to prevent. The numbers above are reported only as evidence
of sample size, not as a performance result.

## Missing prerequisite

A cost-inclusive **rolling** OOS test needs multiple non-overlapping OOS
folds, each with enough trades for `modules/backtest_costs.summarize_r`
(win rate / PF / expectancy / max DD) to be meaningful under three cost
scenarios, plus enough independent candidates for
`modules/reality_check.reality_check`'s multiple-testing correction to have
power. This repo's current data cannot support that for EXP-001's rule:

- The only pair with enough history to even attempt folding (USDJPY)
  produces just 10 total conjunction-rule trades across 20 years, i.e. a
  single 30% OOS slice already has only 7 trades — not enough for one fold,
  let alone several rolling ones, and not enough to run three cost scenarios
  and draw a stable per-scenario conclusion.
- Every one of the 10 matches is LONG; the pre-registered "mirror condition
  for shorts" has zero occurrences in 20 years on this pair, so the short
  side of the rule is untestable here regardless of sample-size fixes
  elsewhere.
- The other 33 pairs cannot be evaluated at all under a *real* historical
  FA gate (no cached rate history), and their live-fetch window (~200–280
  days) is the same window in which the signal_scanner.py docstring for the
  simpler RSI-only version of this filter already recorded 0–4 trades per
  pair — a conjunction with MACD on top of that will not improve on that.

None of this is fixable by retuning the rule; it is a data-depth problem
(missing long-run historical price + point-in-time central-bank-rate series
for pairs other than USDJPY, and too few conjunction events even for
USDJPY). Acquiring/caching that history is outside this run's scope
(research/-only, no changes to data fetchers or the production pipeline).

## Status

`hold` — insufficient historical price and point-in-time FA (interest-rate)
data outside USDJPY to run a valid cost-inclusive rolling OOS test of
EXP-001, and even USDJPY's 20-year cache yields too few rule matches (10
total, 0 short) to support folding, three-scenario costing, or a
multiple-testing correction. Revisit if/when long-run price history and
point-in-time central-bank-rate series are cached for more pairs, or when
enough additional USDJPY history accrues to raise the match count.
