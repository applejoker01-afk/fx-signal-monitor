# EXP-001 RSI reversal + MACD confirmation — hold (2026-11-21)

Status: **hold**. No backtest was run and no performance claim is made.

Selected as the first `queued` item in registry order. Before implementing, I
inspected the existing normalized, cost-adjusted backtest
(`run_cost_adjusted_backtest.py`, output `data/cost_adjusted_backtest_latest.json`,
generated 2026-08-24) and the data available to research scripts.

## Why it cannot support a valid test

1. **No rolling OOS.** The harness does one fixed split (IS 70% / OOS 30%) over a
   180-day window. The registry gate requires cost-inclusive *rolling* OOS.
2. **OOS sample too small.** Latest run: 22 pairs with trades, 125 IS and 117 OOS
   trades in total, at most 10 OOS trades per pair. The 2026-09 diagnosis put the
   sample needed to detect a mean of about 0.03R at around 1,900 trades. A
   rolling split of 117 trades would make any result noise.
3. **Daily closes only.** `fetch_history_with_dates` pulls daily Frankfurter
   (ECB reference) closes. No OHLC and no intraday timestamps, so RSI/MACD entry
   timing, fill price, and SL/TP ordering inside a bar cannot be verified. Per
   CLAUDE.md this is a limitation, not a passed test.
4. **Engine is a simplified TA/FA scorer.** `modules/backtest.run_backtest` omits
   the production filters (seasonality, BOJ cycle, overheating downgrade, event
   avoidance). EXP-001's gate would have to be injected into a scorer I may not
   edit (`modules/` is out of bounds), so it needs a research-local engine.
5. **Cost data are scenario constants.** `modules/backtest_costs` gives three
   scenarios (conservative/standard/adverse) but no historical spread series, so
   cost realism is unverified.
6. **Other available history doesn't fit.** `data/usdjpy_daily.csv` (22y) covers
   one pair only. FRED G10 data are monthly-lagged rates plus daily FX. The hourly
   `price_series.json` is a git-snapshot reconstruction (~800 irregular points per
   pair since 2026-05-20), unsuitable for 10-bar RSI/MACD logic. A single-pair or
   proxy result would not meet the registered standard, so none was produced.
7. **Multiple testing.** The registry already counts 33 variants and the formal
   adjustment is not specified for EXP-001. The Reality Check in the harness covers
   pair selection only.

## Missing prerequisites (to move to `queued` again)

- Multi-year daily OHLC (or hourly) history for the target pairs from a source with
  documented timestamps, stored under `research/`.
- A research-local engine with fixed RSI(14)/MACD(12,26,9) rules as registered,
  next-bar-open fills, and R-multiple output using the production SL logic as baseline.
- A historical spread/slippage assumption, run under the three cost scenarios.
- A pre-declared rolling OOS scheme (window lengths and step) and the multiple-testing
  method and trial count.
- Enough expected trades for the chosen windows (order of 10^3 pooled, or a stated
  power calculation).

## Prior informal evidence (not a test)

Registry entries from 2026-08-26 (no costs, no OOS) showed no drift-controlled long
edge and net-losing short variants. They do not substitute for the gate.

Production files, config, thresholds, stars, sizing and workflows were not touched.
